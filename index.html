Overview

A quick read on what I do and how I got here.

A data scientist who likes turning messy, noisy datasets into models people can actually trust and act on.

I currently build forecasting systems at a research unit in Colombo — combining machine learning (LSTM, XGBoost, ensemble methods) with structural simulation techniques to model macro-level time series. Before that, I worked across household survey data, trade data, and geospatial sources, and shipped everything from automated scraping pipelines to public dashboards. I'm most at home somewhere between statistics, code, and a genuinely interesting dataset.
